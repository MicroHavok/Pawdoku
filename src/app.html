How to play

  1. Place exactly one Ry in every row and every column.
  2. Each color region gets exactly one Ry.
  3. Rys can't touch — not even diagonally.

Controls

  • Tap a cell to mark it with an X (tap again to clear).
  • Swipe across cells to X many at once. Start a swipe on an X to erase.
  • Double-tap (or long-press) to place a Ry.
  • Hint explains the next logical deduction and highlights it.

Every puzzle is machine-checked to have exactly one solution that can be reached by logic alone — no guessing needed.
